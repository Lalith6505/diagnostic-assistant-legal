Privacy Policy — Diagnostic Assistant

Last updated: July 28, 2026

What we collect

When you create an account, we store your email address and an encrypted password (via Supabase Auth). When you run a diagnostic, we store the text you submit and the response generated, tied to your account, so you can search your own history later.

How your data is used

Text you submit for diagnosis is sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a response. It is not used to train any model on our side, and is not shared with any third party except the AI provider needed to generate your result, and Stripe for billing (which never sees your diagnostic content — only payment details).

Data retention

Your query history is retained until you delete your account. You can request deletion at any time by emailing diagnosticassistant.help@gmail.com.

Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated data (including your email address and diagnostic history) at any time by emailing diagnosticassistant.help@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my account." We will process deletion requests within 30 days and confirm by email once your data has been removed from our systems.

Permissions this extension requests

  • storage — to keep you signed in between popup opens
  • contextMenus — to add the "Diagnose this" right-click option

This extension does not read or modify the content of the pages you visit. It only acts on text you explicitly select and submit.
